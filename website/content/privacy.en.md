### In short

This is the privacy policy of the binateam.site website. The site collects nothing: there is no sign-up, no forms, no ads, no analytics and no visit counters. Every Bina Team app has its own policy — for sBoard it is published on the sBoard page.

### Who runs the site

The site is run by the Bina Team. Questions about data: sboard.app@gmail.com.

### What the site keeps in your browser

• Your chosen language and theme — in the browser's localStorage, so the site does not ask again. These settings never leave your device.
• Copies of the site's pages — in the Service Worker cache, so the site opens without internet. The cache holds only the site's files, none of your data.
• The site uses no cookies.

You can remove all of this in your browser settings: "Clear site data" for binateam.site.

### Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub may log technical details of requests — such as the IP address — for security and to run the service. We do not receive these logs. See the GitHub privacy statement: docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

### Outside resources

The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts or counters. Its links lead to GitHub (releases, source code, GitHub Issues) and open your mail app for e-mails. Those resources follow their own rules. Posts in GitHub Issues are public — do not put passwords, codes or other personal data in them.

### E-mails

If you e-mail us, we receive your address and the text and use them only to reply. We do not share them with third parties.

### Children

The site is not aimed at children and collects no data about them.

### Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
